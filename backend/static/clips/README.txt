DROP YOUR VIDEO CLIPS HERE
==========================

Paste any football match video clips (.mp4, .mov, .avi) into this folder.

The backend will automatically detect them and make them available
in the PitchIQ "Extract Moment" panel as selectable clips.

Supported formats: .mp4  .mov  .avi  .mkv  .webm

After adding clips, restart the backend server and refresh the app.
